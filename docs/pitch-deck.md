# Prasadam.com pitch deck

The story-style pitch deck is kept as a Claude Slides deck. Download it as PowerPoint or PDF from the deck’s **Share → Export** menu and save the file here as `docs/Prasadam_Pitch_Deck.pdf` before publishing, if you want it in the repository.

## Story outline (16 slides)

1. **Cover** – Prasadam.com: the temple’s blessing, delivered home. Muhurat launch, 3 Nov 2026.
2. **Meet Ramesh** – 62, retired in Bengaluru, can no longer travel to Shirdi. And Priya, who sends prasad to her parents.
3. **What Ramesh can do today** – India Post, temple websites, faith-tech apps, marketplaces: each trades trust for convenience.
4. **The problem** – No single, trustworthy way to get authentic prasad at home; no low-effort way for temples to send it.
5. **The opportunity** – $70 Bn faith economy; 25,797 postal prasad packs in Telangana; 7.67 lakh Shirdi laddu packets in one festival; ₹147 crore of Sabarimala aravana in a season.
6. **Introducing Prasadam.com** – Devotee website, temple dashboard, admin panel.
7. **The prototype** – Live map, temple cards and prasad menus.
8. **How it works** – Order → notify → pack → ship → deliver and pay weekly.
9. **Trust is the product** – MoU before listing, temple is the seller, FSSAI on every pack, auto-unpublish on MoU expiry.
10. **Competition** – Only player scoring 5/5 on both prasad focus and trust.
11. **Business model** – ₹400 order, ₹200 to the temple, ₹70 contribution; break-even near 4,300 orders a month.
12. **How the margin grows** – Festival and gift packs, subscriptions, temple software fee, NRI shipping.
13. **Roadmap** – Validate, pilot, grow, scale, each with an exit test.
14. **Risks** – Temple onboarding and thin margins first; how we handle each.
15. **The ask** – [₹__ lakh] for a one-state pilot, plus temple and courier introductions.
16. **Back to Ramesh** – This festival, the temple comes to him.

Sources for every figure are in the BRD and the market workbook in this folder.
